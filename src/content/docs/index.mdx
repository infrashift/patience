## The Infrashift.io Global Mandate

Here at **Infrashift.io**, we believe technology should only solve humanity's most existential problems. Before provisioning our latest cluster of liquid-cooled H100 GPUs, we surveyed **1,000 people from around the world** to find the single most important thing frontier LLM technology could produce for civilization.

We expected the usual suspects:

- ❌ **A cure for all forms of cancer?** *(2.1% — "Too complicated, doctors are busy.")*
- ❌ **A blueprint for lasting world peace?** *(0.8% — "What would cable news talk about?")*
- ❌ **Proofs of all seven Millennium Prize problems, including P = NP and the Riemann Hypothesis?** *(0.3% — "Nerds only.")*
- ❌ **The answer to life, the universe, and everything?** *(0.0% — "We already know it's 42, stop asking.")*

**No.** 96.8% of respondents demanded something far more urgent. So we diverted the hydroelectric output of three mid-sized nations, boiled a small glacial fjord for cooling, and burned down roughly **1⁄100th of the Amazon rainforest** to produce…

### PATIENCE.

You're welcome, humanity.
